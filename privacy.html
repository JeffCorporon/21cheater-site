Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Short version: 21 Cheater does not collect or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores

21 Cheater saves your settings (table rules and drill options) and your training progress (accuracy, streaks and which hands you miss) on your iPhone only, so your progress is there next time you open it. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet to work. We do not sell or share data because we never receive any.

Apple

If you install a beta through TestFlight or download the app from the App Store, Apple handles that distribution under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share crash reports or feedback with developers in your device settings or in TestFlight, Apple may provide that information to us so we can fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in your iPhone’s settings.

Deleting your data

You can clear your progress at any time from the app’s Stats screen, or remove everything by deleting the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

21 Cheater is a blackjack training app intended for adults. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
